<h1 align="center">Buch Lab @ Duke</h1>

<h3 align="center">
  Machine Learning for AI-Guided Precision Neuropsychiatry
</h3>

✨ The **Buch Lab at Duke University** develops and deploys machine learning for AI-guided precision neuropsychiatry.

🔬 We integrate neuroimaging, behavioral, clinical, genomic, and environmental data to understand individual differences and heterogeneity across **brain, behavior, and biology**. We develop computational methods, including **cluster-aware and multiview learning**, to identify reproducible, biologically meaningful dimensions and subtypes across psychiatric and neurological disorders.

🧬 Our goal is to bridge **neuroscience, engineering, genomics, and medicine** to advance precision diagnosis and treatment.
